For three seasons the archivist Wen Shu had catalogued the Jade Pavilion without once being noticed, which suited her perfectly.

Then she opened a letter that had been sealed for twenty years, and the quiet prince who lived in the east wing began appearing at her desk every evening with tea she had not asked for.

He said he only wanted the letter back.

He did not explain why he kept staying after she returned it, or why the palace guards had started to call her "the prince's scholar".

When the old Empress Dowager summoned her to the inner court, Wen Shu realised the letter had never been about love at all…
